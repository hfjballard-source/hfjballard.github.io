Bookadoodle Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Bookadoodle does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your library, book progress, settings, and any parent narration recordings stay on your device. Bookadoodle does not upload them to a server or send them to anyone else.

Microphone permission

Bookadoodle requests microphone permission only when a parent chooses to record narration for a book. The microphone is not used for any other purpose. You may decline or revoke this permission in Android settings; only narration recording will be unavailable.

Imported books

Parents choose which PDF and EPUB files to import. Those files remain on the device unless a parent deletes them from Bookadoodle.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be shown in the app before the change takes effect.
